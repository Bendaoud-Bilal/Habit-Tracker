How This System Works

Progress Bar
Tracks your daily habit completion percentage, computed from your live Notion data.
Week Overview
Get a clear snapshot of your entire week, including daily progress and completion rates.
Full Month Overview
Track your habits across the full month, pulled live from your Notion database.
Habit Streak Heat Map
Visual calendar showing your daily completion percentage with color coding — from red (0–20%) to green (85–100%).
Streak Milestones
Celebrate your perfect days when you complete every habit. Build momentum and unlock milestones!
Habit List
Mirrors the checkbox columns in your connected Notion database. Each habit carries an icon you choose from the picker — powered by OpenMoji.

Customization Tips

  • To add/remove/rename habits — edit the checkbox columns directly in Notion, or use the Add Habit form here
  • Checking a box here writes straight back to your Notion database
  • Each habit needs its own unique icon — pick one from the picker when adding or editing a habit
  • Data is never stored in this browser — every load re-reads Notion live

Database

This tracker reads and writes directly to your Notion database via a local backend. One row per day, with a checkbox column per habit, a computed progress value, notes, and status. Nothing is cached in the browser — refresh at any time to see the latest state of your Notion data. Habit icons are stored locally in emoji.json, alongside habit weights in weights.json, since Notion checkbox properties can't hold either.
