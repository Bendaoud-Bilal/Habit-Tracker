How This System Works

Progress Bar
Tracks your daily habit completion percentage, computed from your live Notion data.
Week Overview
Get a clear snapshot of your entire week, including daily progress and completion rates.
Full Month Overview
Track your habits across the full month, pulled live from your Notion database.
Habit Streak Heat Map
Visual calendar showing your daily completion percentage with color coding — from red (0–20%) to green (85–100%).
Streak Milestones
Celebrate your perfect days when you complete every habit. Build momentum and unlock milestones!
Habit List
Mirrors the checkbox columns in your connected Notion database.

Customization Tips

  • To add/remove/rename habits — edit the checkbox columns directly in Notion
  • Checking a box here writes straight back to your Notion database
  • Data is cached locally for offline use — changes sync to Notion when online

Database

This tracker reads and writes to your Notion database via a backend API. Data is cached locally for instant loading and offline use — changes sync automatically when you're online. One row per day, with a checkbox column per habit, a computed progress value, notes, and status.
