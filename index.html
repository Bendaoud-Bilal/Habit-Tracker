How This System Works

Progress Bar
Tracks your daily habit completion percentage. Each day shows a visual progress bar to help you hit your goals.
Week Overview
Get a clear snapshot of your entire week, including daily progress and completion rates to help you stay consistent.
Full Month Overview
Track your habits across the full month. Quickly identify patterns and areas for improvement.
Habit Streak Heat Map
Visual calendar showing your daily completion percentage with color coding — from red (0–20%) to green (85–100%). Spot your consistency patterns at a glance.
Streak Milestones
Celebrate your perfect days when you complete all 10 habits. Build momentum and unlock milestones!
Habit List
Track up to 10 core habits across productivity, health, and personal growth — all in one place.

Customization Tips

  • To change your habits — Edit the habit names in data/habits.json and reload
  • To reset all data — Click the reset button in the footer or clear localStorage
  • Your data is saved automatically in your browser's localStorage
