# ✨ Habit Tracker ✨

<p align="center">
  <b>A premium, glassmorphism-styled habit tracking web application.</b>
</p>

Track your daily habits with dynamic heat maps, real-time analytics, streak milestones, and progress bars. Designed with a sleek dark theme to help you build consistency, one day at a time.

</div>

---

## 🚀 Features

*   **🗓️ Habit Streak Heat Map**: A visual calendar showing your daily completion percentage with an intuitive red-to-green color coding system. Spot your consistency patterns at a glance!
*   **📊 Real-Time Analytics**: Integrated with `Chart.js` to provide instant, real-time feedback. Includes a 14-day Trend Line Chart, a Habit Performance Bar Chart, and an Overall Month Donut Chart.
*   **⚖️ Importance Bands**: Habits aren't all equal. Assign weights to your habits (`Low (0.5x)`, `Normal (1x)`, `High (2x)`) to accurately reflect their impact on your day.
*   **🔥 Streak Milestones**: Celebrate your perfect days! Build momentum and unlock milestones as you complete all your tracked habits.
*   **✏️ Full CRUD Operations**: Dynamically Add, Edit, and Delete your habits directly from the beautifully styled UI.
*   **💾 Local Persistence**: Your data is securely saved automatically in your browser's `localStorage`.

## 🎨 Design System

The application features a modern, Notion-inspired dark theme (`#0f0f13` base) utilizing:
- **Glassmorphism**: Cards feature subtle blur and transparency effects (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`).
- **Micro-animations**: Smooth hover effects and transitions across all interactive elements (checkboxes, table rows, action buttons).
- **Responsive Grid**: Adaptive layout that gracefully resizes for different screen dimensions.

## 🛠️ Built With
- **HTML5** & **Vanilla CSS3**
- **Vanilla JavaScript (ES6)**
- **[Chart.js](https://www.chartjs.org/)** (Real-time analytics visualization)


3. *That's it!* No build tools or backend servers are required. Your data is managed locally.
